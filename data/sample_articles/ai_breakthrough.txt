Scientists at a leading research lab have developed a new artificial intelligence system
capable of solving complex mathematical theorems that previously stumped human mathematicians
for decades. The system, trained on millions of mathematical proofs, uses a novel
reinforcement-learning approach where it receives rewards for logically valid proof steps.
Researchers say this could accelerate discoveries in fields from cryptography to physics.
The team published their findings in Nature, and the model is expected to be open-sourced
by the end of the year. Critics warn, however, that the system still struggles with
problems requiring creative intuition rather than pattern recognition. Several universities
have already expressed interest in integrating the tool into graduate-level research programs.
